regia

regia is a private tool made by Riccardo Cereghino. It publishes short videos to social accounts that he owns, such as Heard in Japan (@heardinjapan) on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and Threads.

It is not a public service. Nobody else can sign in to it.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026.

  • regia only connects to accounts that Riccardo owns. It uses Google and Meta sign-in to get permission to upload videos, read their statistics and moderate their comments.
  • The access tokens are stored in Riccardo's password manager. They are never shared or sold.
  • regia reads public comments on its own videos to hide spam. It does not collect, store or share personal data about viewers beyond what is needed for that.
  • regia does not use advertising, tracking or analytics on this page.
  • To remove regia's access, open your Google account's third-party connections or Meta's business integrations and remove "regia".
